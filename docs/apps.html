On your phone, browser and TV

One server, three ways in, one design: Mallard for Android, the web app in any browser (installed on an iPhone's home screen, it looks and works as the Android app does), and the wall display on a TV or tablet.

Mallard, for Android

The native app: every screen of the web app, controls on the lock screen and in the notification, a home-screen widget, and your phone as a Lyrion player of its own (This phone). A 64-bit phone with Android 8.0 or later.

  1. InstallDownload Mallard for Android on the phone and open it; Android asks once to allow installing from your browser.
  2. Find the serverThe app looks for it on your Wi-Fi by itself. If it doesn't appear, type its address (192.168.1.20:3390).
  3. UpdatesA newer version is offered on Home and in Settings → System; Update opens the download in your browser, and Android installs it over the old one, keeping your settings.

The web app — an iPhone, or any browser

Open http://<server>:3390. On an iPhone or iPad, Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen installs it full screen with Mallard's mark, no browser bars. It has the Android app's screens and Settings, and its own bottom bar: Menu, Home, Library, Search, Now playing. (Playing on the phone itself is the Android app's; the web app plays to your Lyrion players.)

The wall display

Open http://<server>:3390/display on a TV, a tablet or an old laptop: what is playing, huge, with the artist's photograph, biography and the album review, related albums, and the track's music video when a YouTube key is set (free). It rotates through them by itself. Switch it on in Settings → Wall display, which also chooses what it shows and can put the Shelf on it instead.
