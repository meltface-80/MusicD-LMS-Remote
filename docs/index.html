MusicD LMS Remote

Your Lyrion library, rediscovered — on your phone, your browser and your wall

A companion for Lyrion Music Server that runs beside it in Docker. It turns your library into something to browse and rediscover — album of the day, label of the week, Smart Picks, a record-shop Shelf — plays it to any of your Lyrion players, and puts it on a phone (Mallard, for Android), in any browser, and on a TV as a now-playing wall. Away from home, your phone becomes a Lyrion player of its own over Tailscale.
