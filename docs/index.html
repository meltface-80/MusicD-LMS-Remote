Mallard
for Lyrion Music Server

A different face for Lyrion, with more it can do

Mallard is a new way to use Lyrion Music Server: its own interface, on an Android phone, in any browser and on a TV as a now-playing wall, with features Lyrion doesn't have — album of the day, label of the week, Smart Picks, a record-shop Shelf, merged box sets, dynamic playlists — and your phone as a Lyrion player of its own, at home and away. It runs beside Lyrion in Docker; nothing is installed in Lyrion.
