Features

Everything works from your existing Lyrion server over the network: your music files are never written to, and nothing needs installing in Lyrion. The web app and Mallard have the same screens.

Discovery

🌅A Home that changes every day

The date and a greeting, then Random Album and the Album of the Day, Label of the Week, albums not played in six months, what you played recently, and your Library in the order you browse it. Choose and reorder the rows in Settings → Home Screen.

🔎Smart Picks

Six albums a day by artists you don't own: five from the corner of music your library lives in, and one stretch two steps further out — each with the reason it was picked. Play one at once; keep it with a tap. Off until you switch it on.

📻Random album radio

When a player's queue runs out, whole random albums from your library keep it going — not a shuffle of single tracks. It stands aside while Lyrion's own Don't Stop The Music is on.

🏷Record labels

Label of the Week, every label's releases, logos from Discogs and FanArt.tv, and labels merged when one name is spelled two ways. Off until you switch it on.

The Library

🗂Sort and Focus

The whole collection in one grid, sorted by album, artist, genre, year, date added, most or last played, or at random. Focus narrows it — never played, not in six or twelve months, genre, decade, label, source, format, sample rate, bit depth, a first letter — and a name filter finds an album or an artist as you type.

⚡Dynamic Playlists

Save a sort and a focus — say, 90s jazz you've never played — as a playlist that keeps itself up to date as your library and your listening change. Play or queue it whole, with its own order and length.

💿Album pages

Large artwork, every track with its full credit, the year, label and length, the review from Lyrion's Music & Artist Information plugin, Pitchfork's score where there is one, and each artist a link.

🎤Artist pages

The artist's photograph and biography, and every album you have by them; bands list their members and musicians their bands.

🧩Merge a box set

When Lyrion lists a multi-disc set as separate albums, select them and Merge: one album everywhere, its discs in order. Unmerge from Merged albums in the menu.

✏️Edit an album, rescue its artwork

Change an album's title, artist, year or cover, kept in the app's own database: your files and Lyrion stay untouched. Albums with no cover get one found for them.

🎚Format on the artwork

Every cover can carry its format — 24/96, 16/44.1, or the file type of a lossy one — with hi-res picked out in brass (Settings → Appearance).

🔍Search

The whole library by album, artist or label at once, and Pitchfork reviews; with Lyrion's Qobuz plugin, albums you don't own that Qobuz can stream too.

The Shelf

🗄Your library as a record shop

Flick through the spines, narrow by genre, artist, decade, format, label or favourites, turn a case over and play it — full screen in the web app at /shelf, in Mallard, or on the wall display. Off until you switch it on in Settings → Shelf.

Playing

▶Play, queue, play next

Every album's Play Now carries a menu: Play next, after the current track, or Add to queue. Long-press albums or tracks to select several and play, queue, favourite or add them to a playlist at once.

🔊Audio Devices

Every Lyrion player as a card with what it is doing, and each one's own page: switch it on or off, random album radio, rename it, and Lyrion's settings for it — crossfade, volume levelling, Don't Stop The Music, sync.

🦆This phone

Mallard plays on the phone itself, as a Lyrion player of its own, at home and away — This phone.

📝Playlists

Play the playlists saved in Lyrion, add albums or tracks to new or existing ones without disturbing what's playing, and delete one you made (it asks first: that one is permanent).

📤Share a playlist

A short code to paste in a message: whoever receives it imports the tracks their library has, with anything missing listed, not dropped.

And

💚Favourites

Your own favourites, kept by the app — local or streaming albums — and mirrored into Lyrion's own Favorites.

🖼Share cards

Any album, or what's playing, as a picture to share: the cover, the review and its score. The same card from Mallard and the web app.

🎨Themes

Mallard's graphite and brass, and Dark, Light, Copper dark and Brass light, in Settings → Appearance.

⚙Lyrion's settings in the app

Lyrion's own settings pages and every plugin's settings open in the app, and plugins install from it (Plugins).
