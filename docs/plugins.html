Plugins, radio and streaming

Lyrion does the streaming, through its own plugins. The app shows every plugin's menus, installs and updates plugins, and puts your streaming albums beside your own.

Apps & radio

The side menu's Apps & radio browses every Lyrion plugin — Qobuz, TIDAL, Spotty, radio, podcasts, whatever you have — with no special code for any of them: ▶ plays, ⋯ adds Play next and Add to queue. In Mallard they are the Apps and Radio screens.

Installing plugins

Settings → LMS server → Plugins lists what is installed and what can be: install, update or remove one, then restart Lyrion when asked. Third-party plugins need your agreement once, as in Lyrion's own page. Add a developer's repository there too, to try their plugins.

Your Qobuz and TIDAL albums in the Library

Albums you favourite in Qobuz or TIDAL appear in the Library beside your own files once Lyrion's Online Music Library has imported them, and the app notices Lyrion's imports by itself. Settings → LMS server says how many there are, or exactly why there are none. Search also finds albums you don't own that Qobuz can stream: play them, or + to add them to your Qobuz favourites.
