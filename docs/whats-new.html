What's in v1.0.87

The first release of MusicD LMS Remote as it is now — the server, the web app, the wall display and Mallard v0.2.0 for Android, one design across all of them. More people using it is the best way to find what still needs work: how to report a problem.

Help test these

New in this release, and least tried. Please use them and say what you find:
  • This phone — Mallard as a Lyrion player of its own, at home and away from home: Opus 256 kbps at 24-bit/48 kHz or bit-perfect, gapless, Lyrion's volume levelling on the phone, the phone's own volume.
  • Going away and coming home — from Wi-Fi to mobile data and back, Mallard follows on its own.
  • Tracks kept ahead on the phone, for a dead spot or to save data (your own music files; see which tracks).
  • A USB DAC on the phone, bit-perfect.
  • Tailscale built in — in the server and in Mallard; no app to install, no ports to open.

In this release

  • Mallard v0.2.0 — the Android app: every screen of the web app, lock-screen and notification controls, a widget, Audio Devices, the Shelf, every Lyrion plugin, installing plugins, This phone, a USB DAC, and updates offered in the app.
  • One look — Mallard's graphite and brass, Young Serif and Manrope, in the app, the web app and this site; four other themes in Settings → Appearance.
  • Discovery — album of the day, label of the week, Random Album, not played in six months, recently played, Smart Picks, random album radio.
  • The Library — the whole collection, sorted and focused (genre, decade, label, format, sample rate, bit depth, listening history), saved as Dynamic Playlists.
  • The Shelf — the library as a record shop's racks.
  • Audio Devices — every Lyrion player with its power, random album radio, name and Lyrion's own settings, in both apps.
  • Plugins — every Lyrion plugin's menus in the app, and installing, updating and removing plugins.
  • The wall display — a TV or tablet as a now-playing screen.
  • Kind to Lyrion — it reads less while Lyrion scans, and logs what it was asking whenever Lyrion stops answering (/api/lms/outages), to help find why a server restarts.

Known limits

  • A Qobuz or TIDAL track can be held ahead on the phone only one track at a time: Lyrion hands its players one track ahead, and those tracks are not files to fetch. Your own music files are kept twenty ahead.
  • Keeping your own files ahead reads them from the music folder mounted into the server's container (how); without it the server asks Lyrion's download, which some servers never answer.
